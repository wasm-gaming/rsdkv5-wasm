## The common part (all the engines with a settings screen)

- **engine-specs `^0.3.2` → `^0.6.0`**. The app imports the helpers from `../engine-specs.js`, the
  runtime copy the Makefile already ships beside the SDK.
- **English and Spanish**:
  - every string of the app (today Spanish, inline) moves into one `{ en, es }` table;
  - the table is read through `host.locale ?? resolveLocale()`;
  - SDK errors stay English and are shown as a detail under the app's own words.
- **Pause menu**: Continuar, the engine's entries, **Ajustes**, **Pantalla completa**, Volver al
  lanzador (UX.md rule 4).
- **Ajustes**: `createSettingsScreen()`.
  - Rows come from `settingsModel()` over the SDK's `config()` / `options()` descriptors (only those
    with a `label`), plus `SHARED_SETTINGS`.
  - A live change is applied at once.
  - A non-live one is saved and passed at the next start; its row says "Se aplica al reiniciar".
  - Choices are saved per engine with `saveSettings('rsdkv5')` and read at mount with
    `loadSettings()`.
- **Fullscreen**: `createFullscreenToggle(root)` (Cmd+Enter / Alt+Enter, Select + Y), plus the
  pause-menu entry.
- **Performance**:
  - `createWakeLock()`, held while playing when "Mantener la pantalla encendida" is on (default
    on);
  - `createPerfCounter()` when "Mostrar rendimiento" is on: fed once per emulated frame where the
    SDK reports frames, else counting display frames.
- **Controls and audio pacing**: per engine, below.
- **Checks**:
  - `make test` / build;
  - in Chromium, with the owner's test ROMs where needed: the pause menu, every setting applied (or
    noted as on restart), saved across a reload, fullscreen by key and by menu, the counter, en / es;
  - a phone width.
- **Release**: minor.

## Walkthrough (2026-11-18 01:40 UTC) — RSDKv5 done

Adds: what was built, what was settled from the plan's "to check" items, and the checks. It is
RSDKv4's and RSDKv3's version (same record in those repos).

**Settled**:
- **The bump crosses 0.4 and 0.5 without changes**.
- **Controls: yes, live.**
  - The SDK writes `[Keyboard Map 1]` into `Settings.ini` (Windows virtual-key codes: arrows, A / S
    for A / B, Z / X for X / Y, Enter, Tab), so the defaults are known.
  - Input goes through Emscripten's SDL2, so BlastEm's way applies (`src/rsdkv5.controls.ts`). The
    replayed keys carry `keyCode`, which is what this engine maps from (Tab added to that table).
  - The controls are the d-pad, A, B, X, Y, Start and Select. C and Z, keyboard-only aliases with no
    pad button, stay as the engine has them.
- **No other setting gets a label.** `vsync`, `language` and `region` are engine indices with no
  names in the SDK. A wrong label would be worse than none, so they stay internal for now.

**SDK**:
- `config.renderFilter` (`'pixelated'` by default as today, live, Vídeo "Suavizado");
- `RSDKV5_CONTROLS`, `controlTables()`, `remapPad()`, `play.setControls()`;
- engine-specs `^0.3.2` → `^0.6.1`.

**App**: the same changes as RSDKv4's and RSDKv3's apps:
- en / es, the slot screen's own words included ("Cargando partidas…", "menús de Mania", the saves
  error). `findGameScreens()` returns a `key`, and its test follows;
- **Ajustes** and **Pantalla completa** in the pause menu;
- saved in OPFS `settings/rsdkv5.json`;
- the Wake Lock and the counter.

**Checks**:

  - the pause menu (es and en at 390px);

  - → on Suavizado writes `{ renderFilter: 'smooth' }`;
  - the controls swap reaches the SDK;
  - the counter and Alt+Enter;
  - after a reload, the boot config and the controls are back.
- Not run: the app's own slot screen (`loadGame: 'app'`), whose strings changed only by moving into
  the table.
